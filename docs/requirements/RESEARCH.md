# Research requirements for the greenfield lifecycle

The October 7, 2026 [practitioner findings](/Users/mikewolfd/Work/corpora/session-reports-2026-10-07/data-processing-interviews/FINDINGS.md) prioritize verified reuse, durable completion of expensive phases, and useful operational progress. The plans apply those requirements while preserving greenfield freedom. Existing successful implementations are behavioral examples, not mandatory dependencies.

The first delivery must establish: **an unchanged or interrupted job reuses every qualified completed phase, preserves exact data and evidence, and explains what work remains.** A complete final dataset is insufficient if late failure repeatedly destroys expensive intermediate work.

## Evidence and limits

The interviews are practitioner reports with retained incident evidence. They did not rerun jobs or independently audit every cited source. Some issues were fixed in their stated scope; other recovery behavior and root-cause attribution remained unknown. The research does not establish that replacing the whole stack, adding an index, or choosing a particular catalog will improve end-to-end cost.

## First delivery and expansion

Define foundational identity and schema meaning upfront, then deliver the fixed-schema Comments path: exact selected input → restoration/processing → subject and receipt/evidence preparation → validation → publication → independent readback. Use retained fixtures and private qualification first. Include supported resume, progress/resource observations, minimum phase retention, and faults at each costly boundary.

The first delivery passes only when every qualified completed phase is reused where its dependencies and trust scope remain valid, every required incomplete/invalidated check executes, all data and evidence match the independent oracle, and the operator can see remaining work and reuse/rerun reasons. Known immutable inputs permit early completion lookup; unknown or mutable identities must still be established.

Use those measurements to remove confirmed duplicate reads and validation, then qualify R4 changes and R5 dependent refresh. Broader R3 evolution/enrichment and R6 optimization follow while preserving the same recovery guarantees. This sequencing changes delivery priority, not permission to omit foundational identity, publication safety, or retention protection.

Ordered streaming is a measured candidate when population, ordering, identity, and policy are verified. Ineligible input requires an explicitly correct fallback or refusal; a changed row position must not inherit unrelated receipt history. Index construction is likewise a measured choice, not an automatic prerequisite.

## Preserved guarantees and open decisions

Preserve literal source values, selected generations, original receipt history, ordering and duplicates, null/empty distinctions, explicit missing/refused outcomes, and failed-attempt evidence. Independent public readback remains necessary when its trust boundary differs from local validation. Logging explains repeated work; durable phase results and usable resume prevent it.

Product targets, phase granularity/recovery windows, shared-capacity limits, component choices, and measured performance remain open. The research supplies failure cases and priorities; it does not supply invented speedups or operating thresholds. Historical fixed examples must remain labeled as examples rather than newly discovered current defects.
